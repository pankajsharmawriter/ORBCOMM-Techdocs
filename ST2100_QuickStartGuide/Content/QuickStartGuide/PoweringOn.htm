Powering on the device

Once installation is complete, apply power to the unit.

 

  1. Confirm the Power LED turns solid green within 10 seconds.

  2. Allow up to 5 minutes for the GPS LED to acquire a satellite fix.

  3. Confirm the Comm LED turns solid blue, indicating the device is transmitting data to the ORBCOMM network

 

Do not disconnect power during the initial GPS acquisition phase, as this may delay satellite fix by several minutes.
